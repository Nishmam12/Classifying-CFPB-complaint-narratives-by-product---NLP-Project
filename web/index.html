Bonus Implementation

Ensemble Model: Complementary Fusion

Demonstrating how soft-voting fusion between fine-tuned BERT Base and TF-IDF Logistic Regression outperforms every individual model by correcting orthogonal failure modes.

Orthogonal Error Profiles

BERT excels at complex contextual dependencies and syntactic relationships, whereas Logistic Regression operates on high-dimensional exact n-gram surface forms. Their error correlation is remarkably low (r = 0.38), creating ideal conditions for ensemble gain.

Soft-Voting Calibration

Weighted probability blending: P(c) = 0.75 * P_BERT(c) + 0.25 * P_LR(c). This preserves BERT's semantic mastery on nuanced categories like Student Loans while benefiting from LR's sharp discriminative boundaries on keyword-heavy classes.

+0.73 pp Macro F1 Boost

The ensemble achieves 0.7720 Macro F1 and 86.24% Accuracy on the 303k held-out test set, setting a new benchmark over both standalone BERT (0.7647) and standalone Logistic Regression (0.7367).

Error Analysis & Interpretability

Confusion Dynamics & Word Clouds

Empirical discovery: The primary confusion is Money Transfer → Bank Account (14–17%), driven by shared lexical tokens (account, bank, deposit, funds) rather than Debt Collection ↔ Credit Reporting (7–8%).

Figure 1: Normalized Confusion Matrices. Shows high diagonal dominance in BERT Base with remaining off-diagonal dispersion concentrated in Money Transfer → Bank Account.
Figure 2: Salient Vocabulary Distribution. Lexical overlap between Bank Account and Money Transfer explains why linear models confuse wire transactions with regular deposits.
Figure 3: Accuracy vs Latency Frontier. Logistic Regression yields 96% of BERT's performance with a 1,300x lower inference latency, making it ideal for high-throughput streaming.
Figure 4: Imbalance Strategy Delta. Proves that class weighting damages deep neural networks (-7.9 pp) by skewing false positives, while uniquely saving tree models (Random Forest +24.3 pp).
